Getting Started

  1. Approach a laptop and open the OS via the ACE interaction.
  2. Log in with your username and password. Root/admin can see all files.
  3. Use the dock on the left to launch Files, Text Editor, Network, Browser, Calendar and Settings.
  4. Drag windows by their title bar; minimise, maximise or close from the top-right buttons.
  5. Open Network to connect to a wireless router in range.
